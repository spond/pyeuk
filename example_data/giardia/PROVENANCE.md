# Provenance: Giardia duodenalis MLST Benchmark Panel

This benchmark dataset comprises **17 clinical and veterinary isolates** sourced from NCBI BioProjects **PRJNA498263**, **PRJNA41819**, and **PRJNA41821**.

### Benchmark Cohort Composition

1. **Assemblage A (10 specimens: `G_AI_01`–`05`, `G_AII_01`–`05`)**: Sub-assemblages AI and AII sharing beta-giardin (*bg*) alleles with sub-assemblage variation at *tpi* and *gdh*.
2. **Assemblage B (5 specimens: `G_B_01`–`05`)**: Genetically divergent anthroponotic lineage.
3. **Assemblage E (2 specimens: `G_E_01`–`02`)**: Veterinary outgroup.
